    Welcome

    Beam and Ethereum in one wallet: BEAM, ETH, WBEAM and tokens, and the official Beam bridge between them, both ways. Seeds and keys stay in this browser.

    Your seed phrase

    Write these 12 words down, in order, on paper. They restore both parts of the wallet: Beam (in any Beam wallet) and Ethereum (also in MetaMask or Rabby). Closing this popup keeps them until the wallet is created.

      The Beam part starts from Beam's published snapshot (~320 MB, kept 3 days for the next wallet) instead of syncing millions of headers: about two minutes.

      Restore from a seed phrase

      12 words of a Beam wallet restore both parts: the coins are found in Beam's recovery snapshot (~320 MB, the mobile wallet's "automatic restore"), and the same words give the Ethereum accounts. 12 or 24 words of an Ethereum wallet (MetaMask, Rabby) restore the Ethereum part only.

      Open a Beam backup file

      A wallet.db saved from Settings → Backup (or the 0.1 wallet's backup). It opens with the password it had when it was saved; the history comes with it.

      Import an Ethereum private key

      One Ethereum account, from a key exported from another wallet (64 hex characters). The key is kept encrypted in this extension.

      Setting up the Beam part

      The wallet imports Beam's snapshot in the background; this popup may be closed.

                Wallets

                Security

                Only the open wallet's Beam part runs: its regular addresses are online while it is unlocked. The other wallets take payments to their offline, public and max-privacy addresses all the time; their regular addresses wait until they are opened. Locking takes Beam offline (a transfer still being made finishes first). Closing the browser locks it.
