    Beam

    The first in the list is used. When it stops answering for a minute, the next ones are tried from the top down. Drag to change the order, click one to see what is behind it.

    What our two nodes are

    wss.bumblebeam.org is our own Beam node, on our server, reached through our WebSocket door. It is the fastest, and it sees our pool's blocks first.

    wsb.bumblebeam.org is the backup door, run by Cloudflare, not by our server. It passes the wallet through to BeamMW's public nodes and sunpool's, in a random order, with our node last. It keeps the wallet working when our server is down.

    A browser can only open WebSockets, so a node works here only with a WebSocket door (wss). Beam's plain node ports (8100, 10000) do not.
