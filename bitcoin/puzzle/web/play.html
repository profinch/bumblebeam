Play the puzzle

Pick a puzzle, press Start, and your browser starts guessing keys. It turns each number into a Bitcoin address and compares it with the address of the puzzle, as fast as your graphics card or processor allows. The small puzzles were solved long ago, so they make a fair test: you will find their keys for real and feel the work double with every bit. The search pauses when the tab is in the background.

Puzzle

The key to puzzle #N is a number with N binary digits, so each step up doubles the haystack. The square shows the key as 64 hex digits. Almost all of them are known zeros, and only the last few are what the search has to guess. It goes through the range in order, from the smallest key upwards, so it finds a key near the start first. On puzzle #71, it starts at a random place instead and goes on from there. Start small, and you will see a real win in seconds.
