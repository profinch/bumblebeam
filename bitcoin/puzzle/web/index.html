About

The puzzle was made in 2015 with one transaction to 256 addresses; in 2017 the creator moved out #161–#256 and raised the rest tenfold, and in 2023 tenfold again. #N now holds about N/10 BTC. In 2019 the creator also spent a little from every fifth address from #65 on, which put their public keys on the chain.

This site reads all of it from a Bitcoin node of our own, not from someone else's API. The open-source code is in the BumbleBeam repository.

A word of honesty: the lower open puzzles are a race with farms of GPUs. The calculator shows what a home card can expect, electricity included. Play for the fun of it, not for the money.
