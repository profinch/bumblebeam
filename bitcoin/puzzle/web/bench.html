How fast is your device?

Pick your graphics card or your processor, press Run, and see how many keys per second your browser can check. It runs the very search of play and the pool: every key becomes a point on the secp256k1 curve, then goes through SHA-256 and RIPEMD-160, and the result is compared with the addresses being searched for (on a graphics card, with the ten of the pool at once). On a graphics card, it first fits the engine to the card (how many keys a step and how many threads), which play and the pool then use. The graphics card engine first checks itself against the plain JavaScript one the processor runs, so every number is for correct work only. And a result goes in the table only with its proof: the server hides four keys in about five seconds of work at your speed, your run must find them all, and the server times it itself.
