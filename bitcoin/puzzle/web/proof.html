Proof of the finds

What this page proves

When a browser in the pool finds the key of a puzzle, it first makes a commitment: sha256(key ‖ address ‖ salt) of the key, the payout address of the finder and 32 random bytes (the salt), timestamped by the public OpenTimestamps calendars. Then it sends its own claim through MARA Slipstream, and only then tells our server. The commitment is published here at once. It gives nothing away.

Once the prize is spent, nothing is left to steal, and the rest is published: the key, the salt, the piece it was found in and the claim transaction. Verify checks all of it again in your browser, with nothing taken on trust from our server: the key opens the address of the puzzle, it lies in that piece, the commitment is sha256(key ‖ address ‖ salt), the .ots file stamps that commitment, and the claim paid the address of the finder its share. Whether the stamp came before the claim, opentimestamps.org tells: drop the two files below there.

Check a receipt

A finder saves the signed receipt of the pool from the card of the find (Save the receipt). Drop that file here, or choose it: this page checks the signature of the pool on it in your browser, and finds the find on this page.
